# Attribution and reuse

MechLaren was a five-person course project at Seoul National University. The design-review presentation lists 안준표, 윤지훈, 이승현, 이준석, 장민준 as the team members. Repository maintenance by one member does not imply sole authorship of the team deliverables.

The project used course-provided hardware and reference materials. In particular, `firmware/manipulator/follower_modified/follower_modified.ino` is a modified version of the 2025 CED workshop follower example and uses the DYNAMIXEL ecosystem. The workshop distribution itself is not included. CAD files may retain references to standard or vendor components used in the design.

The selected project files are shared here as a historical engineering portfolio. The source archive did not supply a repository-wide license. No MIT or other blanket open-source license is asserted.
